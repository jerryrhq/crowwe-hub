About Crowwe

A digital ecosystem designed around how people actually live and do business.

African consumers and merchants often move between separate apps for conversations, payments, discovery and business records. Crowwe is designed to bring those everyday journeys closer together.

It combines social connection, messaging and calling with payment capabilities, business tools and intelligent assistance through INNA. The result is a simpler path from meeting a customer to completing the transaction and keeping the record.

Trust by design
